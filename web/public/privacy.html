Trail Seek · Privacy Policy

Test build · last updated 18 October 2026

Trail Seek is an experimental outdoor companion app built by a small team in Kerala. This policy describes what the current test build does with your data. It will be revised before any public release.

What stays on your phone

  • Your account (name, email, a salted password hash), avatar, saved trails, settings.
  • Recorded activities and GPS tracks, XP, badges.
  • Photos you attach to community posts and your avatar photo. The avatar is generated on the device; the photo is not uploaded.
  • Downloaded map tiles.

What is shared, and with whom

  • Your group, over Bluetooth, only during a Digital Rope session: your display name, member number, a heartbeat, quick actions (I'm OK, Wait for me, Need help) and, when available, your last GPS position. This never goes through a server.
  • Map tile servers (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap) receive the tile coordinates you view, like any map app.
  • Nothing else. There is no analytics, no advertising SDK and no cloud account in this build. If a backend is added later, this policy will change first and you will be asked again.

Permissions

  • Location: to record treks and share last-known position with your group.
  • Bluetooth: the Digital Rope.
  • Camera / photos: QR join codes, avatar and post photos.
  • Notifications: safety alerts.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account & data removes everything from the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

support@trailseek.app

Safety

Trail Seek is an experimental companion tool, not a safety device. Keep doing manual headcounts and follow your guide and forest department rules.
